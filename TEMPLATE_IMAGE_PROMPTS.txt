================================================================================
FUNNY YOU - 40 TEMPLATE THUMBNAIL PROMPTS  (for Grok / Grok Imagine)
================================================================================

One prompt per template. Paste a PROMPT block into Grok exactly as it is. Grok
takes a single prompt and has no separate negative field, so every exclusion
is already written into the sentence - do not strip anything out, especially
the clauses about the face being visible and unobstructed.

SAVE AS: the filename given on each block, e.g.
assets/templates/astronaut.jpg. The app picks the file up automatically - no
code change needed. Ids match lib/data/templates.dart exactly.

SPEC: portrait 3:4, at least 900px wide, saved as JPG under about 200KB each
so the catalogue stays small in the app bundle.

THE ONE THING THAT MATTERS MOST: the face must sit in the UPPER THIRD of the
frame. The picker overlays the user's own photo as a circular bubble there, so
a face placed lower in the frame gets covered up by it.

WHY THE PEOPLE LOOK OLDER: these tiles are the shop window for a Danish boomer
audience, and they mirror the video prompts, which specify the same
late-sixties character. The user's own face is swapped in afterwards.

1 / 40   Astronaut   [Heroes]
save as:  assets/templates/astronaut.jpg
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT
  An active, stylish person in their late sixties with a full head of grey or
  silver hair and a lived-in, characterful face with real age lines and
  natural skin texture, as an astronaut in a white spacesuit with the helmet
  visor fully open and raised so the whole face is visible, standing on the
  grey dusty surface of the moon holding a small Danish flag on a short pole,
  Earth hanging low in the black sky behind. Caught mid-action: they shove the
  flagpole down into the moon dust and let go with a flourish. Exactly one
  person in the entire photograph, with no other people anywhere. Cinematic
  medium shot with the surroundings clearly visible and the person placed
  naturally in the environment. Their head and face sit in the UPPER THIRD of
  the frame, large, clearly lit, sharp and completely unobstructed in a
  natural three-quarter view. They are absorbed in what they are doing and
  look slightly off to one side, not at the camera and not posing for it.
  Nothing crosses in front of the face: no mask, no sunglasses, no hand, no
  prop and no hair over the features. A warm, natural, characterful
  expression. Photorealistic editorial photograph, vertical 3:4 portrait
  format, very high resolution, rich natural cinematic lighting, filmic colour
  grade, shallow depth of field, crisp detail and sharp focus on the face,
  shot on a 50mm lens at f/2. No text, no watermark, no logo, no caption and
  no border.
