How it works

Three taps. That is the whole thing.

1

Take a selfie

The camera checks the light before you shoot and tells you if the room is too dark. After the photo it checks your head angle, how close you are and whether it is sharp, and says how much to fix. Advice only. You can always keep the photo.

2

Pick a scenario

Deadlift the whole gym. Own the karaoke stage. Escape the care home in a rocket powered wheelchair. Tap one and you see the clip first, with the face locked, before you decide.

3

Watch yourself

We make a five second video with your face in it. Save it, send it to the group chat, put it on whatever you like. It is yours.
