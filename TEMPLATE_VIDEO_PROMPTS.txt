================================================================================
FUNNY YOU - 40 TEMPLATE PROMPTS
Cinematic, activity-driven, nobody looking at the lens, nobody talking.
================================================================================

Every block is COMPLETE and copy-paste ready. Nothing to attach or assemble.
Each scenario gives an IMAGE PROMPT and NEGATIVE for the still, then a VIDEO
PROMPT and NEGATIVE for the clip. The VIDEO PROMPT is standalone: it restates
the identity reference, describes the whole scene and plays the activity out
beat by beat, so it works on its own.

The ids and titles below match lib/data/templates.dart exactly, so the file
names line up with what the app and backend already expect.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THESE ARE WRITTEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. NOBODY LOOKS AT THE CAMERA. The person is absorbed in what they are doing
and never addresses the lens, never poses for it, never waves at it. Looking
down the barrel is what made the earlier set feel stiff and lifeless. Every
scene now has them watching the thing they are actually doing.

2. NOBODY SPEAKS OR SINGS. Mouth closed throughout, no lip movement. Talking
heads look wrong without audio and the mouth is where generated video falls
apart first.

3. CINEMATIC, NOT A PORTRAIT. Medium-wide, surroundings visible, natural
light, filmic colour, shallow depth of field, the person placed in a real
environment. Modelled on generated_video.mp4.

4. EVERY CLIP IS A JOKE IN THREE BEATS. Setup, then it goes wrong, then they
recover their dignity or give up gracefully. The flag springs back out of the
moon dust and floats off. The sword spins the knight the wrong way round. The
officer tickets his own patrol car. Five seconds is enough for exactly three
beats and no more, so every action below is built to that shape. A single
gesture held for five seconds is what made the earlier set boring.

5. THE COMEDY IS PHYSICAL AND BIG. Props rebel, weather interferes, animals
turn up uninvited, and things are consistently heavier, louder or more
powerful than expected. This is slapstick played straight by somebody with
real dignity, which is the bit that makes it funny rather than silly. They
laugh, grin and react openly, and they are allowed to enjoy themselves.

6. NO AGE IS SPECIFIED. The body is generated from the uploaded photograph, so
each user gets their own age and build.

TRADE-OFF WORTH KNOWING: a face in three-quarter view and not held to camera
gives the model fewer clean facial pixels to work from, so identity is harder
to hold than with a face pointed straight down the lens. Check likeness on the
first few renders. If it slips, the fix is a face pass over the finished clip,
not a change to these prompts.

NOTE ON NEGATIVES: with a step-distill speed LoRA at CFG 1.0 the negative
prompt is skipped entirely and has no effect. Anything that MUST be excluded
needs a full-CFG run (cfg 6, 25 steps).
