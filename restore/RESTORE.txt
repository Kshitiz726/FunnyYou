1. WHAT THIS PROJECT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Flutter app ("Funny You") where one selfie produces a funny 5-second video of
the customer inside a pre-made scenario clip. Target audience is Danish boomers,
so the app is Danish-first. 40 scenarios are planned; the app already has all 40
cards in lib/data/templates.dart. What is missing is the template video clips.

  Flutter app      lib/
  Python backend   backend/            FastAPI, talks to ComfyUI on a RunPod pod
  Graphs           backend/workflows/  ComfyUI API-format JSON
  Template clips   backend/templates/  <id>.mp4 plus <id>.points.json
  This kit         restore/

Nothing of value lives only on the pod. Graphs, points, clips, tools, backend
and app are all in this repository. The pod holds model weights and an installed
Python environment, both rebuildable from this document. Losing the pod costs
download time, not work.


2. WHAT THE PIPELINE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One render = TWO queued ComfyUI prompts, not one graph.

  Stage 1  wan_animate.json     Wan 2.2 Animate. Regenerates the head only, at
                                720x1248, 81 frames, 16 fps (~5s).  ~13 min.
  Stage 2  reactor_boost.json   ReActor + GFPGAN face restore over stage 1's
                                output.                              ~6 min.

They are separate prompts ON PURPOSE. Both sets of weights resident at once
exceeds the container's 62 GB memory limit and the server is OOM-killed at the
final encode. Do not "simplify" this into one graph.

Why Wan Animate at all: it drives the head from the template's pose, so hair,
head shape and expression all come across. It regenerates pixels ONLY inside
character_mask; everything outside is the template's own footage, untouched.
That is the entire mechanism by which the costume is preserved.

A third graph, reactor_image_swap.json, makes the small still previews on the
template tiles in the app. It is fast and unrelated to video quality.

           WHEN YOU REDEPLOY, EXPOSE 8188 AND SKIP THE TUNNEL ENTIRELY. The
           tunnel was a recurring source of "Unexpected error" in the app.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    python backend/tools/add_template.py path/to/clips --url http://127.0.0.1:8188

For each video it copies the clip to backend/templates/, uploads it to ComfyUI,
runs a pose probe on frame 1, derives SAM seed points from the 68 facial
landmarks, and writes <id>.points.json.

The filename is the template id and MUST match an id in lib/data/templates.dart.
All 40 cards already exist there with titles, taglines and Danish strings, so
naming the file astronaut.mp4 is the entire registration step.

against the wrong subject. That gate is deliberate -- see the first bullet in §8.

paused first. Before trusting a batch, run it on pirate.mp4 and compare its

8. HARD-WON FACTS. DO NOT RE-LEARN THESE.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* SAM points are PER TEMPLATE. They are pixel coordinates of the render
  resolution. Reusing one clip's points on another silently locks the segmenter
  onto a bystander or the background -- a WRONG render, not a failed one.
  Sidecars are stored normalised 0..1 so they survive a resolution change.

* GrowMask.expand and BlockifyMask.block_size are ALSO in render-resolution
  pixels. Going 480x832 -> 720x1248 means scaling them by 1.5.

* VALIDATE MASKS ACROSS THE WHOLE CLIP, NEVER FRAME 1. A mask covering 13% of
  frame 1 covered 25% averaged over the clip and swallowed the hat entirely.
  Checking one frame is exactly how a broken mask shipped to the customer.

* A BIGGER MASK IS NOT A SAFER MASK. Wan invents content to fill whatever area
  it is given. Growing the mask to 31% of frame made it hallucinate a white
  bandana and age the face into a different man. Tightness is what to optimise.

* THE TEMPLATE'S FACIAL HAIR BECOMES THE CUSTOMER'S. Any beard left OUTSIDE the
  mask is a boundary Wan has to blend into, and it grows a matching beard on a
  clean-shaven reference to make that blend work. Cover the beard completely or
  expect to inherit it.

* WATCH WHAT THE TEMPLATE CHARACTER DOES BEFORE TUNING ANYTHING. The pirate
  REMOVES HIS HAT at ~4s, revealing long grey hair and a full grey beard. Half
  the difficulty of that clip lives in those two seconds.

* Wan Animate at cfg 1.0 -- which the distill LoRA forces -- IGNORES THE
  NEGATIVE PROMPT ENTIRELY. The positive prompt still works. Do not spend time
  writing negatives for this graph.

* VHS_LoadVideo reads from ComfyUI's INPUT folder, never output. Stage 2 must
  have stage 1's file uploaded to input first, or it fails "Invalid video file".

* VHS_VideoCombine with audio wired writes TWO files: name.mp4 (silent) and
  name-audio.mp4. Pick deliberately.

* OPTIONAL GRAPH INPUTS FAIL SILENTLY WHEN UNWIRED. Sam2Segmentation accepted a
  graph with coordinates_negative unconnected, reported no error, and discarded
  every negative point for weeks. IF A SETTING SEEMS TO DO NOTHING, CHECK IT IS
  ACTUALLY CONNECTED BEFORE TUNING IT.

* Iterate on masks with a small preview graph (SAM -> GrowMask -> BlockifyMask
  -> DrawMaskOnImage -> VideoCombine), never full renders. ~1 minute each
  against ~20 for a render. Eleven variants cost less than one wrong render.

* pkill -f "ComfyUI/main.py" MATCHES ITS OWN SSH WRAPPER and kills your shell.
  Kill by PID.

* Identity/sharpness trade off along ReActor boost visibility. Measured on the
  same stage 1 output:
      GFPGAN 1.0, raw source     identity 0.823  sharpness 797
      GFPGAN 0.7, source @1024   identity 0.843  sharpness 550   <- shipped
      GFPGAN 1.0, source @1024   identity 0.815  sharpness 908
  0.84 is this pipeline's ceiling. 0.90 mean has never been reached; the best
  ever recorded on any template is 0.880 (elf). The customer asked for >90%;
  that has been reported to them as not currently achievable.

* Render cost: ~20 min per 5-second video (13 stage 1 + 6 stage 2). Sampling is
  only 8 steps x ~58s; everything else is model loading, SAM and pose. The app's
  progress display now names those phases because they look like a hang.


9. THE OPEN PROBLEM (state as of 2026-08-23)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Customer complaint: "look at the beard, does my face have that kind of beard and
hair". Both defects are understood. Neither is confirmed fixed.

  a) THE BEARD. Wan gives the customer a heavier moustache and a chin patch the
     reference does not have. Confirmed to originate in Wan itself, NOT the
     ReActor polish: the raw stage 1 output already has it. Mechanism is the
     boundary-blend bullet in §8 -- the template pirate's grey beard sits
     outside the mask and Wan grows a beard to blend into it.
  b) THE HAT. Destroyed near the end. The mask swallowed it, so when the
     character takes it off, Wan renders a hallucinated black pompadour.

ROOT CAUSE of (b) and a contributor to (a): coordinates_negative was NEVER
wired into Sam2Segmentation. Every negative point -- including the one holding
the hat out of the mask -- was silently discarded.

Config B contains the fix:
    - coordinates_negative wired to the PointsEditor's second output
    - GrowMask.expand reduced 60 -> 24
    - pirate.points.json extended with positives covering the whole beard
Mask previews over all 81 frames confirm it keeps the hat, keeps the coat, and
holds 15-38% coverage every frame with no dropouts. NOT RENDERED YET.

REJECTED ON EVIDENCE -- DO NOT RETRY:
  - Union of the SAM mask with a grown pose mask (~31% coverage). Rendered as
    R1: invented a white bandana and aged the face. Worse than the bug it fixed.
  - Pose-derived mask alone: drops to 0.5% coverage on frames where DWPose
    loses the face, leaving the original character's head untouched.
  - Extra "more hair" positive points: SAM jumps to the full body (66-72% of
    frame) and the costume gets re-imagined. Widen with GrowMask instead.

UNTRIED IDEAS, in the order worth trying:
  1. Render config B and measure. Everything else is speculation until this is
     done.
  2. Feed Wan a TIGHT HEAD CROP of the customer's photo instead of the raw
     selfie. The uploaded reference is 400x400 with the face only a small patch
     of it, which is a weak identity signal. my_face_head.png in the project
     root is such a crop. A render to test this (R2) was queued and lost when
     the pod paused. This is generic and would help every customer.
  3. The positive prompt still works at cfg 1.0 -- adding explicit appearance
     words is a legitimate lever. Not generic across customers, so treat it as
     a per-template fallback, not a fix.
  4. ReActorMaskHelper / ReActorSetWeight can widen and strengthen the swap
     region in stage 2, which is the only stage that can overwrite a chin the
     first stage got wrong.

NOTE ON TEMPLATE CHOICE: an old bearded man with long grey hair who removes his
hat mid-shot is close to a worst case for this pipeline. Template framing drives
quality more than any setting. Prefer clips where the character's head is
already close in shape and hair to a typical customer.
